Okay, that title really isn't fair. NethServer has all the Linux stuff, it's just<br />
that you don't have to interact with it in the traditional way in order to<br />
reap the benefits. NethServer is a web-based management software package<br />
built on top of CentOS. You can download it as a separate distribution,<br />
