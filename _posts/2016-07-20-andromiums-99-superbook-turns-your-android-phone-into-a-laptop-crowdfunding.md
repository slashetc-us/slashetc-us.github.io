<p>Smartphones have changed the way the world gets online, by letting you put a tiny, touchscreen computer in your pocket or handbag. But sometimes it&rsquo;s still nice to have a larger screen, a full-sized keyboard, and a mouse or touchpad.</p>
<p>The Superbook is a smartphone accessory that gives you all those things. It looks like a laptop, but all the processing power and software comes from your phone.</p>
<p>Plug your Android smartphone into the Superbook with a USB cable and you can interact with your Android apps in a notebook-like environment.</p>
