<p>Researchers in the past have exfiltrated information through air gaps by blinking all sorts of lights from LEDs in keyboards to the main display itself. However, all of these methods all have one problem in common: they are extremely noticeable. If you worked in a high-security lab and your computer screen started to blink at a rapid pace, you might be <em>a little concerned</em>. But fret not, a group of researchers <a href="http://cyber.bgu.ac.il/advanced-cyber/system/files/LED-it-GO_0.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">has found a new light to blink</a>&nbsp;(PDF warning). Conveniently, this light blinks &ldquo;randomly&rdquo; even without the help of a virus: it&rsquo;s the hard drive activity indication light.</p>
<p>All jokes aside, this is a massive improvement over previous methods in more ways than one. Since the hard drive light can be activated without kernel access, this exploit can be&nbsp;enacted without root access. Moreover, the group&rsquo;s experiments show that &ldquo;sensitive data can be successfully leaked from air-gapped computers via the HDD LED at a maximum bit rate of 4000 bit/s (bits per second), depending on the type of receiver and its distance from the transmitter.&rdquo; Notably, this speed is &ldquo;10 times faster than the existing optical covert channels for air-gapped computers.&rdquo;</p>
<p>We weren&rsquo;t born last night, and this is not the first time we&rsquo;ve seen information transmission over air gaps. From <a href="http://hackaday.com/2016/07/01/bridging-the-air-gap-data-transfer-via-fan-noise/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">cooling fans</a> to <a href="http://hackaday.com/2013/02/25/using-a-flashing-lcd-monitor-to-transfer-data/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">practical uses</a>, we&rsquo;ve seen air gaps overcome. However, there are also&nbsp;<a href="https://hackaday.com/2015/09/01/the-year-of-the-car-hacks/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">plenty of &ldquo;air gaps&rdquo;</a>&nbsp;that contain more&nbsp;copper than air, and require correspondingly&nbsp;less effort.</p>
