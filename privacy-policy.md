# Privacy Notice

This privacy notice discloses the privacy practices for (www.slashetc.us).
This privacy notice applies solely to information collected by this website.
It will notify you of the following:

1. What personally identifiable information is collected from you through
the website, how it is used and with whom it may be shared.
2. What choices are available to you regarding the use of your data.
3. The security procedures in place to protect the misuse of your information.
4. External website links and/or content.

## Information Collection, Use, and Sharing

I (Michael J. Ford) am the sole owner of the information collected on this
site. I only have access to/collect information that you voluntarily give me
via email or other direct contact from you. I will not sell or rent this
information to anyone.

This website honors the [Do Not Track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do_Not_Track)
(DNT) field in popular browsers. If this field is enabled, no data will be collected.

This website only collects IP information to indicate page views. At no time will
this information sold or shared with any third party outside of myself. Any IP
Address collected by this site is two (2) byte anonymized. E.g. a IP will be logged
as 192.168.xxx.xxx.

This website does not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), execpt
where may be explicitly required in a contact form. Any PII collected that you have
personally gave me via a form, e.g. your name or email address, will be used to
respond to you regarding the reason you contacted me and no other reason. I will not
share your information with any third party.

## Your Access to and Control Over Information

You may [opt-out](/opt-out) of IP tracking at any time. If you are using any sort of
tracking detection/blocking tools such as [DNT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Do_Not_Track)
or [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock/) then you are automatticly
opted-out and no further action is necessary.

If you have explicitly given PII, you may opt out of any future contacts from me
at any time. You can do the following at any time by contacting me via the email
address given on my website, or contact form:

* See what data I have about you, if any.
* Have me delete any data I have about you.
* Express any concern you have about my use of your data.

## Security

I take precautions to protect your information. When you submit any information via
the website, your information is protected both online and offline.

Wherever I collect any information, that information is encrypted and transmitted
to me in a secure way. You can verify this by looking for a lock icon in the
address bar and looking for "https" at the beginning of the address of the Web page.

While I use encryption to protect information transmitted online, I also protect
information offline. Only myself has access to information. The computers/servers
in which we store information are kept in a secure environment.

## Links

This web site contains links to other sites. Please be aware that I are not responsible
for the content or privacy practices of such other sites. I encourage my users to be
aware when they leave my site and to read the privacy statements of any other site
that collects personally identifiable information.
