The Outlaw group is conducting an active campaign which is targeting Linux systems in cryptocurrency mining attacks.

On Tuesday, the [JASK Special Ops](https://jask.com/solutions/special-ops/) research team disclosed [additional details](https://jask.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Shellbot-Campaign_v2.pdf) (.PDF) of the attack wave which appears to focus on seizing infrastructure resources to support illicit Monero mining activities.
