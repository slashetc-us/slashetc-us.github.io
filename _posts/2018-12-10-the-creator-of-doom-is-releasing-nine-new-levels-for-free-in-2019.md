John Romero, one of the creators of _Doom_, announced a special surprise for fans on the 1993 game's 25th anniversary: more _Doom_.

Sort of, anyway. Romero doesn't own the rights to Doom as a series, and rights-holder Bethesda Softworks already rebooted the series for a modern audience, [with great success](https://www.metacritic.com/game/pc/doom). But as a fan, Romero has access to the same modding tools as anyone else.

SEE ALSO: [How to play the 'Doom' creator's first new level in 21 years](http://mashable.com/2016/01/15/doom-john-romero-new-level/?utm_campaign&utm_cid=a-seealso&utm_context=textlink&utm_medium=rss&utm_source)

He's putting those tools to work on building out a whole new, nine-level episode as a "megawad," which is basically a collection of fan-made additions and changes (commonly referred to as mods), all packaged together.  [Read more...](https://mashable.com/article/doom-john-romero-sigil-new-episode)
