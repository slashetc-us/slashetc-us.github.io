<p>So, here we are: With a new president come new <a href="http://mashable.com/2016/11/10/twitter-worried-surveillance-state-under-trump/?utm_campaign=Mash-Prod-RSS-Feedburner-All-Partial&amp;utm_cid=Mash-Prod-RSS-Feedburner-All-Partial" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">concerns</a> over surveillance powers that could so forcefully bite us in the ass.</p>
<p>The public has good reason to believe that President Donald Trump would love to <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2016-11-29/fbi-and-nsa-poised-to-gain-new-surveillance-powers-under-trump" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">expand</a> data-collection programs in his administration. He's said, <a href="http://www.cnn.com/2016/06/15/politics/donald-trump-muslims-mosque-surveillance/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">repeatedly</a>, that he'd like to surveil mosques. Rep. Mike Pompeo&mdash;Trump's pick for CIA director who's likely to be confirmed in the next few days&mdash;has also <a href="http://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2016/11/trumps-cia-director-wants-to-return-to-a-pre-snowden-world/508136/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">advocated</a> for expanded spy efforts. All signs point to lots of little electronic eyeballs, watching us always.</p>
