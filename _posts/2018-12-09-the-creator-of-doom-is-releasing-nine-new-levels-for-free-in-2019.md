<p>John Romero, one of the creators of <em>Doom</em>, announced a special surprise for fans on the 1993 game's 25th anniversary: more <em>Doom</em>.</p>
<p>Sort of, anyway. Romero doesn't own the rights to Doom as a series, and rights-holder Bethesda Softworks already rebooted the series for a modern audience, <a href="https://www.metacritic.com/game/pc/doom" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">with great success</a>. But as a fan, Romero has access to the same modding tools as anyone else.</p>
