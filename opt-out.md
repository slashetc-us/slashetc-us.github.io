## If you are not *opted-out*, may I make a suggestion
{: .center-text}

If you are not opted out, and you wish to only disable tracking for my site,
go ahead and opt-out above. However you still may be tracked on other websites.  
{: .center-text}

I **HIGHLY** suggest you enable [Do Not Track](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=do+not+track&t=ffab&ia=web)
in your browser of choice. If Do Not Track is enabled, I will not have any analytics
on your visits (so I won't know if a post I made is good or not -sad panda-), but websites
that actually honor Do Not Track will not track you either.
