<p><img width="406" height="232" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/hackup-hacker-news-desktop-app-406x232.png" alt="hacker news desktop client" />Avid readers of social news sharing site Hacker News might be&nbsp;interested in a new app&nbsp;recently added to Flathub. Called HackUp, it is a&nbsp;Hacker News desktop client written in Vala. It lets you browse and read Hacker News submissions without needing to open a web browser (which&nbsp;for a legendary procrastinator like me, is a good thing). [&hellip;]</p>
